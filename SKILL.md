# Logo Concept Lab

从品牌气质与使用场景选择识别主体，用实际画面筛选构思，选定后再精修和交付。极简允许安静、温暖或有力量；共形、负空间、角色都是可选手法。

Choose the source of recognition from brand character and use, curate actual artwork, then refine and deliver selected work. Minimal design can be quiet, warm, or forceful; shared contours, negative space, and characters are optional.

## 1. 保持本轮范围 / Preserve scope

沿用已有品牌资料、参考与选定方向；缺少影响判断的输入再问，其余明确假设。提供官网时核实相关事实。保留精确字符、既定字体、纯图形要求、数量与交付位置；不擅自加名称、英文、标语或整套 IP。局部修改直接处理，不重跑概念发散。

Reuse established facts, references, and choices; ask only for material missing inputs and state other assumptions. Verify relevant facts on supplied websites. Preserve exact text, approved fonts, symbol-only constraints, count, and destination. Do not add names, translations, slogans, or an IP system. Local edits do not reopen concept exploration.

## 2. 新构思先筛选 / Screen new concepts

简要判断识别来自字标、符号还是组合，以及哪种关系值得探索、为何适合品牌。出图前淘汰只有行业套话、通用图标或寓意拼接的方向；缺少依据先查已有资料，不虚构品牌故事。品牌契合与形式潜力足够即可，不要求行业独占符号或强行猎奇。此时明确探索关系，保留具体轮廓、比例与笔触的空间；若提示词仍只有「空间关系、节奏、亲近」等宽泛词，补清哪些部分怎样关联，以及画出来要验证什么。

Identify whether recognition comes from lettering, a symbol, or a combination, which relationship to explore, and why it fits. Before generation, reject category clichés, generic icons, or symbolic ingredient lists without a useful formal idea. Revisit supplied facts rather than inventing a story. Brand fit and formal potential suffice; exclusive symbolism and novelty are not requirements. Define the relationship while leaving contours, proportions, and strokes open. If the prompt still relies on broad terms such as space, rhythm, or warmth, specify which parts relate in what way and what the drawing should test.

多个概念应在主要识别关系上不同；仅换字体表现、色彩或加减辅助图形通常是变体。用户要求变体就给变体，不另行发散。以实际画面筛选，默认推荐 2–3 个有可见依据的方向；用户指定数量优先。默认字体的普通排列、通用图标或扫描框可以留作探索，不能只靠释义晋级推荐。新概念筛选、参考对照和失败记录按需读 [构思与视觉评审](references/concept-review.md)。

Concept alternatives should differ in their primary recognition relationship; changes in type treatment, color, or auxiliary symbols are usually variants. Deliver variants when requested. Screen actual artwork and normally recommend 2–3 directions with visible evidence; follow the user's requested count. A default font in ordinary layout, a generic icon, or a scan-frame mark may remain an exploration, but explanation alone does not qualify it as a recommendation. Consult [concept and visual review](references/concept-review.md) for screening, reference comparison, and iteration records.

## 3. 参考参与探索 / Use references in exploration

有少量已认可参考时先实际查看，记下可见的比例、轮廓、负空间或字体目标，以及要避免沿用的第三方轮廓；不凭标题猜。没有已认可参考时，依据现有品牌资料与资产建立暂定视觉目标，并说明这是设计判断，不强制另搜。用户自有的既定标志按保留要求精修。新形态探索未指定 SVG 时，按任务选择图像或草图等视觉路线，不因代码更方便就默认用编码代替设计；图像生成是可选手段，几何构形和现成字体也可按品牌需要使用。位图概念不冒充矢量。无需在释义里写学习他人或来源叙述；用户询问来源时据实回答。出图后回看参考并对照。

When a small set of approved references is available, inspect it and record visible targets such as proportion, contour, negative space, or type, plus third-party contours to avoid; never infer appearance from titles. If no approved reference exists, derive provisional visual targets from the supplied brand material and assets, label them as design judgment, and do not require a new search. Refine a user-owned established mark within its preservation requirements. For new-form exploration without an explicit SVG request, choose an appropriate visual or sketch route instead of defaulting to code for convenience. Image generation is optional; use geometric construction or existing fonts when they suit the brief. Do not present raster concepts as vectors. Keep explanations focused on the current brand and visible result; answer source questions truthfully. Compare against available references afterward.

首轮保留轮廓和比例的探索空间；用户指定的造型、字形或 SVG 要求照做，选定后的局部精修再精准约束。已有矢量的局部改动直接编辑。

Keep initial exploration open around contour and proportion. Honor user-specified forms, letterforms, and SVG requests; make selected local refinements precise. Edit existing vectors directly for local changes.

## 4. 看画面再决定 / Judge the artwork

打开实际输出，检查读名、形式秩序、记忆点与目标尺寸。中文字保留偏旁和阅读线索，英文保留区分字母的结构；错字、伪字或无法确认的字符修复前只能算草图。字标主导时要有可指出的字形或字间判断，不把选字体称为原创字形；字体搭配及不改字体要求优先。

Inspect reading, formal coherence, memorable features, and intended sizes. Preserve essential Chinese components and distinguishing Latin structures; wrong or uncertain glyphs remain sketches until repaired. Leading wordmarks need visible lettering or spacing decisions; font selection is not custom lettering. Font-pairing and protected-font requests take precedence.

遮住说明，先描述画面实际传达的性格，再与品牌目标及可用参考或暂定视觉目标比较；情绪不必被逐字猜中，但不能只靠文案成立。标注这是设计判断，未做观察者测试不声称用户识别通过。用干净背景看核心标识，按用途检查单色、反白及小尺寸；比较光学大小，不拉伸。纯图形画面不加字标、标题、编号或说明。

Without the explanation, describe the character the artwork conveys, then compare it with the brief and any available reference or provisional visual target. Viewers need not guess exact emotion words, but prose cannot supply all character. Label design judgment as such; do not claim observer validation without testing. Inspect the core mark on a clean background and relevant mono, inverse, and small-size states. Match optical size without stretching. Symbol-only artwork excludes wordmarks, titles, numbers, and captions.

按主要问题局部修正轮廓、比例、负空间及图形与中文的关系，并复核目标是否实际改善。保留已成立部分和每版释义；做有限的针对性精修，不无限整张重抽。详见 [构思与视觉评审](references/concept-review.md) 与 [形式与字标](references/minimal-design.md)。

Refine contour, proportion, negative space, and the relationship between the mark and Chinese lettering around the main observed issue; verify that the intended change is visible. Preserve viable elements and the rationale for every version. Keep refinement focused and finite. See [concept and visual review](references/concept-review.md) and [form and lettering](references/minimal-design.md).

## 5. 按需精修与交付 / Refine and deliver as needed

**每个版本呈现后紧接简短释义。** 默认用几句话说明形式选择、实际可见的元素或字形关系、品牌依据和待验证项。释义以成图为准，不复述未实现的提示词或编造故事；不追加学习他人或来源叙述。先看图再解释，文字放在图外。局部修改版说明目标、实际变化和保留项。用户明确要求只看图、不解释时遵守。

**Follow every presented version with a concise rationale.** Explain the form, visible elements or lettering relationships, brand fit, and what remains to validate. Ground it in the actual output rather than unrealized prompts or invented stories; do not append a learning or source narrative. Show the image first, then place the rationale outside the artwork. For local revisions, state the target, visible change, and preserved elements. Honor explicit requests for images without explanation.

按当前问题读取，不一次加载所有参考：

Read only what the current task needs:

- [形式与字标](references/minimal-design.md)：形式选择、中文/英文结构与双语层级。Form, lettering, and language hierarchy.
- [品牌表达](references/brand-expression.md)：科技抽象、情绪转译、角色与延展。Technology, emotion, and optional character systems.
- [可选构形](references/construction.md)：共形、负空间与指定字母融合。Shared contours, negative space, and monograms.
- [矢量与 Figma 交付](references/delivery.md)：选定后的路径重建、导出与回读。Selected artwork reconstruction, export, and verification.

清楚区分探索与推荐，未达标时如实说明，不为凑数推荐弱稿；用户明确要求的数量仍照给并标注状态。输出格式、比例和透明度服从本轮需求；位图概念不能冒充可编辑矢量。说明可见依据、实际文件和未解决问题，不擅自定稿。没有实际对照结果，不声称已达到参考水平。

Separate explorations from recommendations, and report when no concept meets the bar rather than padding recommendations. Still deliver the exact requested count and label each status. Follow requested format, aspect ratio, and transparency; raster concepts are not editable vectors. Report visible evidence, actual files, and unresolved issues without declaring an unapproved final identity. Reference-level claims require actual comparison.
