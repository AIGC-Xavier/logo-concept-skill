# 极简标识设计参考 / Minimal Identity Design

这份参考展开 Logo Concept Lab 的核心设计判断，帮助设计师从品牌自身出发选择标识形式、字标结构和表现强度。它不是固定风格模板，也不要求每个项目都采用定制字标。

This reference develops Logo Concept Lab’s core design decisions: selecting an identity form, wordmark structure, and level of expression from the brand itself. It is not a fixed style template, and it does not require a custom wordmark for every project.

## 范围与路由 / Scope and routing

- 先读取本轮明确的品牌资料、受众、市场、使用场景、参考和保留要求；区分已知事实与设计判断。
- 当用户指定文字、字体或字形规则时，按指定约束工作；不要把本参考解释为改字或改字体的许可。
- 需要识别、比较或精修 Logo 形式与字标时使用本参考。若任务只要求配字体、排版、图标或应用延展，就保持在该范围内。

- Start from the brand facts, audience, market, use cases, references, and preservation requirements provided for this task. Separate known facts from design interpretation.
- Follow explicit instructions about wording, fonts, and letterforms. This reference never grants permission to alter them.
- Use this reference to choose, compare, or refine logo forms and wordmarks. If the task only asks for font pairing, layout, an icon, or an application, stay within that scope.

## 先定品牌性格与主形式 / Start with brand character and primary form

先用品牌要被记住的特质和实际使用条件，判断识别主要应来自哪里。可从以下形式中选择，也可以提出有依据的其他形式：

Use the brand traits people should remember and the real use conditions to decide where recognition should come from. Choose among these forms, or propose another form with a clear reason:

- **字标 / Wordmark：**名称本身承担记忆，或文字的文化、编辑、时尚、历史特征很重要。
- **几何或抽象图形 / Geometric or abstract mark：**结构、空间、秩序、连接或动作比名称形状更能表达品牌。
- **文化或结构关系 / Cultural or structural relation：**地域、工艺、材料、建筑、自然或历史提供了独特的构形关系。
- **图像或有机符号 / Pictorial or organic symbol：**具象形象、手绘性或情绪表达符合品牌性格，并能形成清楚的轮廓。
- **组合标识 / Combination：**图形与文字各自有明确作用，且在实际用途里需要共同出现。

- **Wordmark:** the name carries recognition, or its cultural, editorial, fashion, or historical character matters.
- **Geometric or abstract mark:** structure, space, order, connection, or action expresses the brand better than the name’s letterforms.
- **Cultural or structural relation:** place, craft, material, architecture, nature, or history offers a distinctive formal relationship.
- **Pictorial or organic symbol:** an image, hand-drawn quality, or emotional expression fits the brand and can form a clear silhouette.
- **Combination:** both symbol and name have clear jobs and need to appear together in actual use.

新构思先筛掉通用、又缺少品牌依据的点子；这不要求每个品牌都采用新奇、行业独占或前所未见的符号。不要按行业名称自动选符号或排除符号。具象行业图形并非一概不可用；如果它只是通用行业图标，再判断是否有更贴合品牌事实的表达。

Screen out generic ideas that lack a reason grounded in the brand. This does not require every brand to use a novel, category-exclusive, or unprecedented symbol. Do not select or reject symbols solely by industry label. A pictorial industry cue can work; when it is only a generic industry icon, consider whether another expression better fits the brand facts.

先明确一个主形式，辅助形式只在解决真实识别或使用需要时加入。组合关系可采用并置、上下排列、共用轴线或嵌套，但不预设某一种布局。

Name one primary form first. Add a supporting form only to solve a real recognition or use need. A combination may be side by side, stacked, aligned on a shared axis, or nested; no layout is automatic.

## 选择主要设计动作 / Choose the dominant design move

默认先寻找一个最能承载品牌记忆的设计动作，例如比例、轮廓、字腔、留白、切口、重复节奏、连接方式、笔触或色彩位置。其他处理可以支持它，但不必机械限制为一个动作。

As a default, find one design move that can carry the brand’s memory: proportion, silhouette, counterform, spacing, a cut, repeated rhythm, connection, stroke character, or color placement. Supporting decisions may accompany it; this is not a quota.

当多个显眼处理互相竞争，或需要解释才能看懂时，先降低次要处理，再评估主动作是否仍然有力。外放、实验或手绘表达只有在符合品牌性格时才使用；安静细节也可以构成明确识别。

When several conspicuous treatments compete or require explanation, reduce the secondary ones and see whether the primary move still works. Bold, experimental, or hand-drawn expression belongs where it fits the brand; quiet details can also create recognition.

概念方向首轮说明识别来源，以及预期的结构关系和品牌依据；保持在方向层面，不预先写死轮廓、比例、角度等几何细节，除非用户明确指定。选定或用户批准方向后，围绕观察到的问题局部精修。结构方向改变识别来源或关键关系；风格变体保留原有识别机制，调整字形或字体表现、色彩等。用户明确要求字体或颜色变体时，按要求提供，并遵守“不改字体”等限制。同一字标仅新增或移除辅助符号，先视为组合变体；只有主要识别关系确实改变，才作为另一概念评估。概念比较的完整步骤见[概念评审参考](concept-review.md)。

For a first-round concept, state its source of recognition, intended structural relationship, and brand rationale. Keep this at the direction level; do not prescribe specific geometry such as silhouette, proportions, or angles in advance unless the user specifies them. Once a direction is selected or approved by the user, refine the observed issue locally. A structural alternative changes the source of recognition or a key relationship; a style variant keeps the recognition mechanism and changes type treatment, color, or similar expression. Honor explicitly requested type or color variants, subject to constraints such as “do not change the font.” Treat an auxiliary symbol added to or removed from the same wordmark as a lockup variant; assess it as another concept only when the primary recognition relationship genuinely changes. See the [concept review reference](concept-review.md) for the full comparison process.

参考图应先实际查看，再选取少量与品牌相关的特征进行转译，并在出图后回看与参考的关系，确认没有沿用其可识别轮廓或组合版。按需参考即可，不要求每次搜索新参考或制作大型情绪板。

View a reference before using it. Select a few relevant features to translate, then compare the generated result with the reference to check that its recognizable silhouette or lockup has not been reused. Use references as needed; do not require a new reference search or a large mood board for every task.

## 选定字标时：从结构开始 / For wordmarks: begin with structure

定制程度应由品牌需要决定。不要把现成字体直接称为定制字标，也不要为了显得独特而强行变形。新字标设计应落实到字形、比例或字间关系的具体处理，不能用未调整的字体排字冒充完成了设计；单独的字体搭配任务除外。

Let the brand need determine how much customization is appropriate. Do not call an unchanged font a custom wordmark, and do not distort letters just to appear distinctive. A new wordmark should implement concrete decisions in letterform, proportion, or inter-letter relationships; unchanged typesetting is not evidence of completed lettering design. Separate font-pairing tasks are an exception.

### 中文结构 / Chinese structure

- 先确认每个字不可丢失的骨架、偏旁关系、主笔、字内空间和阅读路径；保留准确字符与词义。
- 比较字面比例、横竖关系、重心、字距，以及相邻汉字如何形成节奏或共用视觉轴线。
- 安静描述的是视觉噪声，不是识别力度或改造幅度。可通过明确的比例、间距、轮廓或字间关系建立鲜明识别，同时保留可读骨架；按品牌需要决定结构改动。
- 每个方向记录核心结构判断，避免所有字都套用相同圆角、粗度或方块轮廓。

- Identify the essential skeleton, component relationships, defining strokes, internal spaces, and reading path of each character. Preserve the exact characters and meaning.
- Compare glyph proportions, horizontal and vertical strokes, visual center, spacing, and the rhythm or shared axis between adjacent characters.
- Quietness describes visual noise, not identity strength or the degree of change. Clear proportions, spacing, contours, or inter-letter relationships can create a strong identity while preserving a readable skeleton; choose structural changes for the brand’s needs.
- Record the structural idea for each direction. Do not force every character into the same rounded, heavy, or square treatment.

### 拉丁字母结构 / Latin letter structure

- 从字母骨架、开口、字腔、端点、笔画对比、大小写和整体字距判断哪些特征应保持稳定。
- 可通过少数关键字母、比例、连接、端点或字距建立识别；不要同时改造每个字母而削弱读名。
- 核对相邻字母的碰撞、空隙与节奏。个别字母的特色应服从整词的可读轮廓。

- Start with letter skeletons, apertures, counters, terminals, stroke contrast, case, and overall spacing to identify what should remain stable.
- Recognition may come from a few key letters, proportion, joins, terminals, or spacing. Avoid altering every letter at once and weakening the name.
- Check collisions, gaps, and rhythm between neighboring letters. A distinctive glyph should serve the readable word silhouette.

### 中英文字标关系 / Chinese–Latin relationship

先判断主要市场、阅读顺序和品牌已有的语言资产，再确定哪种语言主导。两种文字可共享比例、笔画粗细、几何、留白或基线逻辑，不必强求同等大小或同一种字形风格。

Decide the primary market, reading order, and existing language assets before setting hierarchy. The scripts may share proportion, stroke weight, geometry, spacing, or baseline logic; they need not be equal in size or use identical letterform styles.

确认中英文各自清晰可读后，再判断是否需要组合锁定版。根据真实场景选择并置、垂直、共轴或图形旁置；组合版是可选资产，不是 Logo 必须具备的默认形态。

Make sure each language is independently legible before deciding whether a combined lockup is needed. Choose side-by-side, stacked, shared-axis, or symbol-plus-type arrangements for the actual context. A bilingual lockup is optional, not an automatic requirement.

## 有机形与手绘性 / Organic and drawn character

先定义笔触的粗细变化、转角、端点与不规则幅度，再让它们形成一致的轮廓。亲和不等于统一圆角，趣味不等于给通用图标加眼睛；角色的姿态、比例或局部表情应有一个属于该品牌的识别特征。先看单色剪影，细节和强调色只支持它。

Define stroke modulation, turns, terminals, and the degree of irregularity before shaping a coherent silhouette. Warmth is more than rounded corners, and play is more than eyes on a generic icon. Give a character a distinctive pose, proportion, or expression that belongs to the brand. Evaluate its monochrome silhouette first; details and accent color should support it.

## 尊重文字、字体与指定约束 / Respect text, fonts, and explicit constraints

- “不改字体”意味着保留指定字体；不要把它转换为另一种字形设计。
- “不要文字”或“只要图形”时，不增加字标、标题、编号或说明。
- 准确保留用户提供的字符、拼写、大小写和语言版本；有歧义时先依据已给信息，不自行创造品牌文字。
- 用户要求字体搭配时，就给出现有字体的搭配与层级建议；不强行把任务变成定制字标。

- “Do not change the font” means keep the specified font; do not turn it into a different letterform design.
- For “no text” or “symbol only,” add no wordmark, title, number, or caption.
- Preserve supplied characters, spelling, case, and language versions exactly. Do not invent brand wording.
- When asked for font pairing, recommend pairings and hierarchy using fonts; do not force the task into custom lettering.

将字体转为轮廓只改变文件形式，不会自动使字体授权状态更安全。需要使用字体时，沿用项目提供的授权信息；本参考不作授权判断。

Converting a font to outlines changes its file form; it does not by itself make the font’s licensing status safer. Follow the project’s supplied font-licensing information. This reference makes no licensing determination.

## 在实际呈现中评估 / Evaluate in actual presentation

在目标尺寸和背景中查看标识：检查完整名称、单色、反白以及必要的小尺寸状态。关注字腔是否闭合、细节是否消失、负空间是否变形、笔画是否粘连和不同文字是否仍易读。

Inspect the mark at intended sizes and on relevant backgrounds: check the full name, monochrome, reversed color, and any needed small-size version. Look for closed counters, lost details, distorted negative space, joined strokes, and script legibility.

按项目场景选择测试方式；屏幕预览只是视觉检查，不能代替对实际材料、工艺或生产条件的验证。不要把未经测量的时间阈值或未执行的观察测试写成结果。

Choose checks that match the project. A screen preview is a visual review, not validation of materials, processes, or production conditions. Do not report an unmeasured time threshold or an unperformed observation test as evidence.

按问题做针对性修正：错字或错形时修正字形并保留仍成立的结构；字标过于通用时，重看识别来源与字间关系；候选彼此相似时，替换较弱的识别机制；仅有平衡问题时，只调整位置或间距；方案过度接近参考时，重新选择来源或转译方式。避免反复全量重生成；聚焦修改仍无法解决时，说明具体限制或需要的决定。

Route revisions to the observed issue: correct a wrong glyph while keeping viable structure; revisit recognition and letter relationships when type looks generic; replace a weak recognition mechanism when candidates are too similar; adjust placement or spacing when only balance is off; rethink the source or translation when a result is too close to a reference. Avoid endless full regeneration. If focused changes do not resolve the issue, state the specific limitation or decision needed.

## 核心标识与交付 / Core identity and delivery

先交付或评估 Logo 本体。锁定版、辅助图形、网格、口号和应用样机按需要另行呈现，不要让展示版式掩盖核心标识。

Deliver or evaluate the logo itself first. Present lockups, supporting graphics, grids, taglines, and mockups separately when needed; do not let a presentation layout obscure the core mark.

根据用途选择文件格式、画布与背景。透明 PNG 可以作为交付；不强制正方形画布、JPG 预览或纯白底。若声称可编辑矢量、已导出或已通过生产检查，应以实际文件和检查结果为依据。

Choose file format, canvas, and background for the intended use. A transparent PNG is acceptable; a square canvas, JPG preview, or white background is not mandatory. Claims of editable vector, export, or production approval must match the actual files and checks.

## 概念评审 / Concept review

概念比较可按需简要标记识别来源与结构关系，不要求固定结构卡或作为用户交付物。评审时可暂时遮住方案说明，先判断标识传达的品牌情绪，再核对说明是否成立。完整步骤见[概念评审参考](concept-review.md)。

For concept comparisons, briefly note the source of recognition and structural relationship when useful; no fixed structure card or client deliverable is required. During review, optionally hide the explanation first and judge the brand emotion conveyed by the mark, then check whether the explanation holds up. See the [concept review reference](concept-review.md) for the full process.
